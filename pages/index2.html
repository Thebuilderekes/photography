The Glam Process

Three steps to your perfect transformation.

  1. Amount

    The Consultation

    We begin by discussing your skin type, outfit colors, and the "vibe" of your event. Whether it's a wedding, photoshoot or any other type of event, we plan every detail.

  2. 02

    The Transformation

    Sit back and relax while I use premium, skin-safe products to create your look. I focus on long-wear techniques so your glam stays flawless from the first photo to the last dance.

  3. 03

    The Final Glow

    The reveal! We make final touches, set the makeup for 12+ hours of wear, and ensure you feel like the most confident version of yourself before you head out.
